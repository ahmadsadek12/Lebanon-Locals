Your Safety Matters

We're committed to keeping our guests safe and secure

Verified Hosts

All hosts must verify their identity and provide accurate information about their listings.

Property Standards

Listings must meet our safety and quality standards including working smoke detectors and emergency exits.

24/7 Emergency Line

Round-the-clock support available for urgent safety concerns: 961 81 434 070

Before You Book

  • Read the listing description carefully and check all amenities
  • Review the host's profile, ratings, and guest reviews
  • Check the cancellation policy before confirming
  • Ask questions through our messaging system
  • Verify the exact address and location

During Your Stay

  • Familiarize yourself with emergency exits and safety equipment
  • Keep your valuables secure
  • Report any safety hazards to the host and Lebanon Locals immediately
  • Follow house rules and local regulations
  • Keep important documents and emergency contacts handy

COVID-19 Safety

We require all hosts to follow enhanced cleaning protocols and safety guidelines. Hosts must commit to our 5-step enhanced cleaning process between guests.

  • Hosts must wait 24 hours between guest stays when possible
  • Use approved cleaning products and disinfectants
  • Follow local health authority guidelines
  • Provide hand sanitizer and cleaning supplies
  • Properly ventilate spaces between stays

Report a Safety Concern

If you experience a safety issue during your stay, contact us immediately:

  • Emergency: Call 961 81 434 070 (24/7)
  • Non-Emergency: Message us through your booking dashboard
  • After Your Stay: Report issues within 24 hours via your trip page
