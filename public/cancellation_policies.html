Cancellation Policies

Lebanon Locals understands that plans can change. Review our cancellation policies below to understand what happens when you need to cancel a reservation.

Flexible: Full refund 1 day prior to arrival

  • Cleaning fees are always refunded if the guest did not check in.
  • The Lebanon Locals service fee is non-refundable.
  • If there is a complaint from either party, notice must be given to Lebanon Locals within 24 hours of check-in.
  • Lebanon Locals will mediate when necessary, and has the final say in all disputes.
  • A reservation is officially canceled when the guest clicks the cancellation button.
  • Cancellation policies may be superseded by the Guest Refund Policy, safety cancellations, or extenuating circumstances.
  • Applicable taxes will be retained and remitted.

More than 24 hours before check-in: Full refund of accommodation fees

Less than 24 hours before check-in: First night is non-refundable, remaining nights are refunded

After check-in: If you leave early, nights not spent are 100% refundable after the first 24 hours

Moderate: Full refund 5 days prior to arrival

  • Cleaning fees are always refunded if the guest did not check in.
  • The Lebanon Locals service fee is non-refundable.
  • If there is a complaint from either party, notice must be given to Lebanon Locals within 24 hours of check-in.
  • Lebanon Locals will mediate when necessary, and has the final say in all disputes.
  • A reservation is officially canceled when the guest clicks the cancellation button.
  • Cancellation policies may be superseded by the Guest Refund Policy, safety cancellations, or extenuating circumstances.
  • Applicable taxes will be retained and remitted.

More than 5 days before check-in: Full refund of accommodation fees

Less than 5 days before check-in: First night and 50% of remaining nights are non-refundable

After check-in: If you leave early, 50% of nights not spent are refunded

Strict: 50% refund up until 7 days prior to arrival

  • Cleaning fees are always refunded if the guest did not check in.
  • The Lebanon Locals service fee is non-refundable.
  • If there is a complaint from either party, notice must be given to Lebanon Locals within 24 hours of check-in.
  • Lebanon Locals will mediate when necessary, and has the final say in all disputes.
  • A reservation is officially canceled when the guest clicks the cancellation button.
  • Cancellation policies may be superseded by the Guest Refund Policy, safety cancellations, or extenuating circumstances.
  • Applicable taxes will be retained and remitted.

More than 7 days before check-in: 50% refund of accommodation fees

Less than 7 days before check-in: No refund

After check-in: If you leave early, nights not spent are not refunded
