What's Covered

  • Booking Guarantee: If a host cancels your reservation within 30 days of check-in, we'll help you find comparable accommodation or provide a full refund
  • Property Issues: If your accommodation doesn't match the listing description, we'll work to resolve it or provide alternative housing
  • Payment Protection: Your money is held securely until 24 hours after check-in
  • Communication Safety: All booking communications go through our secure platform
  • Review System: Read authentic reviews from verified guests before booking

How to Stay Protected

1. Book Through the Platform: Always complete bookings and payments through Lebanon Locals - never pay outside the platform.

2. Read Reviews: Check host ratings and guest reviews before booking.

3. Communicate on Platform: Keep all communications within our messaging system for your protection.

4. Report Issues Promptly: Contact us within 24 hours of check-in if there are problems.

5. Document Everything: Take photos of any issues and keep records of communications.

Guest Refund Policy

If your host needs to cancel, or your listing isn't as advertised, we'll refund you and may provide a travel credit. In cases of extenuating circumstances (natural disasters, emergencies), special policies apply.
