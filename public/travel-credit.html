What is Travel Credit?

Travel Credit is money you can use toward future bookings on Lebanon Locals. Credits are automatically applied to your account and can be used for accommodations or experiences.

How to Earn Travel Credit

  • Referral Program: Invite friends and earn $25 when they complete their first booking
  • First Booking Bonus: New users receive $15 credit on first booking over $75
  • Host Rewards: Superhosts earn quarterly bonus credits
  • Promotional Offers: Seasonal campaigns and special promotions
  • Reviews: Leave quality reviews and earn periodic credit rewards

Quick Tip

Refer 4 friends and earn $100 in travel credit - enough for a weekend getaway!

How to Use Travel Credit

1. Travel Credit is automatically applied at checkout

2. Credits are applied before other payment methods

3. Any remaining balance can be paid with your preferred payment method

4. Check your credit balance anytime in your account dashboard

Terms & Conditions

  • Travel Credit cannot be redeemed for cash
  • Credits expire 1 year from the date they're issued
  • Credits are non-transferable between accounts
  • Minimum booking amount may apply for certain credits
  • Credits cannot be combined with certain promotional offers

Check Your Balance

You can view your current Travel Credit balance in your account dashboard. Credits are automatically applied when you book qualifying stays or experiences.
