Our Standards

At Lebanon Locals, we maintain high standards to ensure quality experiences for both hosts and guests. These standards guide everything we do.

Quality Standards for Hosts

Listing Requirements

  • Accurate descriptions and up-to-date photos
  • Clear house rules and policies
  • Honest representation of amenities
  • Proper disclosure of any limitations or issues

Safety Requirements

  • Working smoke and carbon monoxide detectors
  • Clear emergency exit routes
  • Fire extinguisher on premises
  • Safe electrical and plumbing systems
  • Secure locks on all doors and windows

Cleanliness Standards

  • 5-step enhanced cleaning process between guests
  • Fresh linens and towels for each guest
  • Sanitized surfaces and high-touch areas
  • Clean bathrooms and kitchens
  • Proper waste disposal

Guest Expectations

  • Treat hosts' properties with respect
  • Follow house rules and community guidelines
  • Communicate clearly and promptly
  • Leave honest, constructive reviews
  • Report issues or concerns immediately

Community Standards

We expect all users to:

  • Be respectful and inclusive
  • Communicate honestly and transparently
  • Honor commitments and agreements
  • Resolve conflicts constructively
  • Follow all local laws and regulations

Review Standards

Our review system is built on trust and authenticity:

  • Only guests who complete a stay can leave reviews
  • Reviews must be honest and based on actual experiences
  • Both hosts and guests can leave reviews
  • Reviews are public and cannot be deleted (only hidden for policy violations)
  • Fake or manipulated reviews are strictly prohibited

Superhost Standards

Exceptional hosts can earn Superhost status by maintaining:

  • 4.8+ average rating
  • 90%+ response rate within 24 hours
  • Less than 1% cancellation rate
  • At least 10 completed stays per year

Report Violations

If you encounter a listing or user that doesn't meet our standards, please report it so we can take action.
