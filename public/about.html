Our Story

Connecting people with authentic Lebanese experiences since 2020

Who We Are

Lebanon Locals was founded with a simple mission: to help travelers experience the real Lebanon through the eyes of locals. We believe that the best way to discover a country is by connecting with its people, its culture, and its traditions.

What started as a small community of passionate locals sharing their homes and experiences has grown into Lebanon's premier platform for authentic travel experiences. We're proud to have facilitated thousands of meaningful connections between travelers and local hosts.

Our Mission

We're on a mission to showcase the beauty, diversity, and warmth of Lebanon. Through our platform, we empower local hosts to share their unique spaces, skills, and stories while providing travelers with unforgettable, authentic experiences they can't find anywhere else.

Why Choose Lebanon Locals?

Unlike traditional booking platforms, we focus exclusively on Lebanon, giving us deep local knowledge and strong community connections. Our hosts are carefully vetted, our experiences are curated for authenticity, and our support team truly understands the local context.

Whether you're looking for a cozy apartment in Beirut, a traditional house in Byblos, or unique workshops like sushi-making, wine tasting, or olive picking - we connect you with the best Lebanon has to offer.
